Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to create an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

Using welcoming and inclusive language.

Being respectful of different opinions and experiences.

Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.

Taking responsibility for mistakes.

Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

Harassment or discrimination.

Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.

Public or private harassment.

Sharing someone's private information without permission.

Any conduct considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action against any behavior considered inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and public spaces where an individual officially represents the community.

Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated fairly and confidentially.

Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these guidelines when addressing violations:

Correction: A private warning explaining inappropriate behavior.

Warning: A formal warning for a violation or repeated inappropriate actions.

Temporary Ban: Temporary restriction from participating in the community.

Permanent Ban: Permanent removal from the community for serious or repeated violations.
